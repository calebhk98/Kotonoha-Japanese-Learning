# Series Plan: マリアのにほんごにっき

## Series Metadata
- **id**: `maria-no-nihongo-nikki`
- **title**: マリアのにほんごにっき
- **description**: Maria, a 29-year-old Brazilian graphic designer, moves to Osaka for a one-year work contract. Each episode follows one small but real challenge of adult expat life — finding an apartment, shopping alone, getting lost, making a first friend — told entirely in N5 Japanese with warm humor and honest emotion.
- **level**: n5
- **tags**: ["adult-learner", "daily-life", "osaka", "expat", "slice-of-life", "n5", "series"]

## Cast
- **マリア・シルバ (Maria Silva)**, 29 — Brazilian graphic designer, protagonist. Warm, curious, stubborn. Laughs at mistakes but gets quietly frustrated when invisible. Journals every night.
- **たなか けんじ (Tanaka Kenji)**, 55 — Landlord, lives below. Gruff but deeply kind. Leaves vegetables without explanation. Speaks slowly without condescending.
- **すずき ゆい (Suzuki Yui)**, 27 — Maria's coworker. Enthusiastic about foreign culture, sometimes overwhelming. Notices when Maria doesn't understand and backs up without making it a big deal.
- **チェン・ミン (Chen Min)**, 32 — Regular at neighborhood konbini, from Shanghai, 6 years in Osaka. Pragmatic, funny, full of survival tips. One-time encounter (do NOT make him recurring).
- **おかだ さん (Okada-san)**, 68 — Vegetable stand owner. Chatty, patient, delighted by Maria's Japanese attempts.

## Episodes

### Ep 1: はじめてのアパート
- **id**: `maria-ep1-apartment`
- Maria arrives with two suitcases, dying phone battery. Meets Tanaka-san. Can't work out the shower. Eats konbini onigiri on the floor feeling alone. Tanaka-san knocks, offers tea, says just 「どうぞ。」She cries a little, writes in journal that she said ありがとうございます correctly.
- **Grammar focus**: これ/それ/あれ, ここ/そこ/あそこ, ～はどこですか, numbers 1-31, は/が/を/に/で particles, すみません/ありがとうございます/おねがいします, basic adjectives おおきい/ちいさい/あたらしい/むずかしい
- **Emotional beat**: Exhausted and scared → small human warmth

### Ep 2: スーパーで
- **id**: `maria-ep2-supermarket`
- First solo supermarket run with handwritten list. Can't find pasta, accidentally buys 2kg rice. Breakthrough: successfully asks where the eggs are and understands the answer. Buys one mystery sweet just because she paid correctly.
- **Grammar focus**: ～をください, ～はどこですか / ～はあそこです, food vocabulary (たまご/ごはん/パン/やさい/にく/さかな/くだもの/ぎゅうにゅう), numbers+counters (ひとつ/ふたつ), いくらですか, colors for packaging
- **Note**: Food vocab here is incidental/transactional. Do NOT make this a preference episode — save likes/dislikes for Ep 4.

### Ep 3: でんしゃに のります
- **id**: `maria-ep3-train`
- First workday commute. Studies map the night before, confident. Gets on wrong train, ends up 3 stations past stop. Meets Chen Min on the platform. His advice: "Just ask. People are nicer than the signs." Arrives 22 minutes late.
- **Grammar focus**: でんしゃ/ちかてつ/バス/えき/ホーム/のりかえ, ～にのります/～でおります/～にいきます, ～までどうやっていきますか, time expressions (なんじですか/～じに/いま/あとで), ～ない form (いきません/わかりません), まず/つぎに/それから
- **Note**: Chen Min is a ONE-TIME encounter. Do not set up future meetings.

### Ep 4: はじめてのともだち
- **id**: `maria-ep4-first-friend`
- Yui invites Maria to ramen. Maria panics about ordering but decides to try herself — goes reasonably well. Over lunch in slow simple Japanese + occasional English word, they discover: both hate cilantro, both love old movies, both find their home cities very loud. Maria has weekend plans for the first time.
- **Grammar focus**: ～をおねがいします/～をひとつください (ordering — background, not the main focus), ～がすきです/きらいです/すきじゃないです, おいしい/からい/あまい/つめたい/あつい, ～はどうですか, hobby vocabulary (えいが/おんがく/りょうり/スポーツ/ほん)
- **Note**: Ordering food is incidental background. The main grammar focus is preference expressions (すきです/きらいです).

### Ep 5: おかだ さんの やさいや
- **id**: `maria-ep5-vegetable-stand`
- Maria has walked past for weeks. Okada-san calls out, holds up burdock root (ごぼう). Longest Japanese conversation yet — vegetables, cooking, weather, families, in sentences of 4-5 words. Maria mentions grandmother grew vegetables (in broken Japanese). Okada-san wraps three burdock roots and refuses money.
- **Grammar focus**: Vegetable vocabulary (ごぼう/だいこん/なす/にんじん/たまねぎ), weather (きょうははれです/あめがふります/さむい/あたたかい), て-form for sequence (たべて/つくって/かって), family vocabulary (かぞく/はは/ちち/そぼ/そふ), ～ています (りょうりしています), days/frequency (まいにち/まいあさ/ときどき/よく)

### Ep 6: にほんごの てがみ
- **id**: `maria-ep6-letter`
- Maria writes Tanaka-san a thank-you note in Japanese. Spends an entire evening, throws away four drafts. Four sentences. Slides it under his door. Next morning: a small plant outside her door and a note in large careful hiragana 「よく かけました。」
- **Grammar focus**: Written polite form (～です/～ます in writing), ありがとうございます/おせわになりました, simple past (～ました/～でした), ～てくれました, humble/polite vocabulary (いただきます/よろしくおねがいします), てがみ/かきます/よみます/ことば
- **Emotional beat**: The trying is the thing that is seen.

## Reviewer Notes (APPROVED)
- Chen Min in Ep3: make clear he is a one-time encounter, do NOT set up future meetings
- Ep4: food-ordering language is incidental; main focus is preference expressions (～がすきです)
- Keep all vocabulary genuinely N5 (common everyday words, basic kanji only: 日月人大小山川 etc.)

## Writing Guidelines
- Sentences: short and clear (AはBです。AがBをします。)
- Dialogue: natural but polite ます/です register throughout
- Kanji: only most basic joyo (日、月、水、火、木、金、土、人、山、川、大、小、中、上、下、年、今、何) — everything else hiragana
- Target length: 350-450 words of Japanese text per episode
- Adult emotional stakes: loneliness, independence, small victories — NOT children's content
- Do NOT use casual register until Ep 4+ and only with Yui
