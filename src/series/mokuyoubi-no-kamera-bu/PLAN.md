# Series Plan: 木曜日のカメラ部

## Cast
- **中村りょうた (Nakamura Ryota)**, 28 — De-facto organizer; design dept. Earnest, intense about photography, bad at reading the room. Club was his idea; proud and terrified it will fall apart.
- **上田さくら (Ueda Sakura)**, 31 — Accounts dept; oldest in club. Dry humor, observant, initially most skeptical. Only joined because Thursdays were too empty. Her photos are unexpectedly tender.
- **伊藤たくや (Ito Takuya)**, 25 — Sales. Joined because Ryota kept asking. Loud in office, camera makes him quiet. Better at this than expected and unsure how to feel about it.
- **山本ゆき (Yamamoto Yuki)**, 27 — HR; newest employee. Gentle, perceptive, asks questions that make people uncomfortable in a good way. Photographs people almost exclusively.
- **大西ひろし (Onishi Hiroshi)**, 45 — Production manager; overheard and Ryota couldn't say no. Owned a film camera in his 20s. Knows more about composition than anyone but shares only when asked. The group's quiet anchor.

## Episodes

### Ep 1: 最初の集まり
- **id**: `kamera-ep1-saisho-no-atsumari`
- First Thursday meeting in a small kissaten. Nobody knows what "photography club" means in practice. 40 minutes discussing what kind of club they want. Hiroshi orders the best thing on the menu. Ryota has a printed agenda. Takuya makes fun of it. Sakura photographs it.
- **Grammar focus**: ～たいと思っている (goals for the club), ～ましょう/～ませんか (inviting participation), group activity vocabulary (集まり/テーマ/撮影/カメラ/フィルム/デジタル), expressing hesitation (～かどうか分かりませんが/とりあえず)
- **Emotional beat**: Awkwardness of voluntary time with coworkers. Nobody is quite themselves yet. Agreement to meet next Thursday feels bigger than expected.

### Ep 2: テーマ：光
- **id**: `kamera-ep2-tema-hikari`
- First real shoot. Theme: 光 (light). Fushimi Inari in late afternoon. Ryota too focused on technical perfection to take many photos. Yuki photographs a stranger's hands in sunlight; the stranger says thank you. Takuya photographs a vending machine and insists it's artistic. Hiroshi says one sentence about Yuki's photo that changes how she thinks about her own eye.
- **Grammar focus**: ～ながら (walking while shooting), ～てみたら (what happened when you tried), photography vocabulary (構図/露出/シャッター/光源/影/ピント), giving/receiving feedback politely (～と思いますが/どうでしょう/なるほど)
- **Reviewer note**: Hiroshi's one sentence should be simple and concrete (N4 level). Don't let his revelation become emotionally complex — keep it a practical observation that lands personally.

### Ep 3: 雨の日曜日
- **id**: `kamera-ep3-ame-no-nichiyoubi`
- Optional Sunday shoot — only Sakura and Takuya show up. Planned Arashiyama; rain changes everything. Shoot under convenience store awnings and covered shopping arcade. Sakura teaches Takuya about patience without framing it as a lesson. Ramen afterward; they talk about things they wouldn't say at the office. Friendship begins here, to both their surprises.
- **Grammar focus**: ～てしまった (plan falling apart: 雨が降ってしまって), ～ばよかった (mild regret: 長靴を持ってくればよかった), ～けど as soft connector in casual conversation, weather/spontaneity vocabulary (予報/仕方ない/せっかく/それでも)
- **Reviewer note**: ～てしまった also appears in Ep 5. Writers must differentiate usage context clearly so it functions as progression, not repetition.

### Ep 4: 写真展
- **id**: `kamera-ep4-shashinten`
- Ryota proposes entering a small local photo exhibition — three photos each, submitted as a club. Not everyone is enthusiastic. Yuki worries her people-photos need permission. Sakura worries hers are too personal. Group has its first real argument — uncomfortable and meaningful. They submit anyway.
- **Grammar focus**: ～なければならない (permissions: 許可をもらわなければならない), ～かどうか (uncertainty: 出してもいいかどうか), expressing disagreement politely (～はどうかと思いますが/でも/～じゃないでしょうか), exhibition vocabulary (応募/出展/審査/締め切り)
- **Reviewer note**: Give the argument a SPECIFIC concrete trigger — photo selection conflict, cost of entry, who gets to pick which photos. Without a concrete trigger the conflict feels manufactured.

### Ep 5: ひろしさんのカメラ
- **id**: `kamera-ep5-hiroshi-san-no-kamera`
- Hiroshi brings a 35mm film camera. Explains — for the first time — that he photographed seriously in his 20s and stopped when he had children. He says he "put it away" (not that he regrets it). Yuki asks if she can photograph him with the film camera. He says yes. The resulting photo becomes one everyone talks about.
- **Grammar focus**: ～ていた (past habitual: 昔は毎週撮っていた), ～てしまった (something that happened, without strong regret overtone), ～てもらえますか (asking politely), talking about the past (昔/あの頃/若い頃/久しぶりに)
- **Reviewer note**: Keep Hiroshi's revelation SURFACE LEVEL at N4. Do not have him elaborate on complex emotions about parenting vs. passion — just the simple fact: he had a camera, he put it away. The reader can do the emotional math.

### Ep 6: 結果発表
- **id**: `kamera-ep6-kekka-happyo`
- Exhibition results: the club won nothing. Ryota more upset than he admits. Sakura's photo receives a written comment from one judge — not a prize, just one sentence. She reads it twice in the bathroom before telling anyone. Group goes out drinking, takes bad karaoke photos of each other.
- **Grammar focus**: ～はずだった (what was expected), ～てよかった (worthwhile despite outcome), consolation language (残念だったね/でも/次は/気にしないで), casual social vocabulary (居酒屋/乾杯/お疲れ/一杯)

### Ep 7: 来週も木曜日
- **id**: `kamera-ep7-raishuu-mo-mokuyoubi`
- Three months after the first meeting. With little discussion they decide to keep going. Hiroshi has developed the film roll. Yuki's portrait of him is among twelve photos; he had it printed at work quietly, gives each member a small copy. Next Thursday's theme announced: 変化 (change). They all have something in mind.
- **Grammar focus**: ～ようになった (how things changed: 気にならなくなった/話せるようになった), ～てきた (gradual development), ～つもり (plans: また来週来るつもり), reflective vocabulary (続ける/これからも/いつの間にか)
- **Emotional beat**: The drama is in the absence of drama. Five people stand outside the kissaten from Ep 1. Nobody photographs the moment. Takuya almost does, then puts his phone away.

## Reviewer Notes (APPROVED)
- Sakura/Takuya dynamic (Ep 3): don't deepen it further in Eps 6-7 beyond warm camaraderie — it's established but doesn't need payoff
- Ep 4 argument: writers MUST give it a concrete trigger (photo selection, cost, permission issue)
- Ep 5: Hiroshi's backstory at surface level only — N4 register
- ～てしまった in Ep 3 AND Ep 5: writers must vary the usage context

## Writing Guidelines
- Compound sentences: 2-3 clauses as norm; single-clause for emotional emphasis
- ~60-70% kanji from N5/N4 lists; hiragana fallback for N3+
- Mix plain form (narrative, inner monologue) and polite form (workplace dialogue, first interactions)
