# Series Plan: 一人暮らし物語

## Series Metadata
- **id**: `hitori-gurashi-monogatari`
- **title**: 一人暮らし物語
- **description**: Natsuki, 23, has just moved to Osaka for her first full-time job. Each episode follows one month of her new independent life — figuring out grocery budgets, loneliness, small victories, and what it actually means to be an adult.
- **level**: n4
- **tags**: ["slice-of-life", "city-life", "independence", "osaka", "workplace", "n4", "series", "adult"]

## Cast
- **田中なつき (Tanaka Natsuki)**, 23 — Protagonist. Optimistic but secretly anxious. Makes lists. From a small town in Mie Prefecture. Over-apologizes at work, under-sleeps at home.
- **木村けんじ (Kimura Kenji)**, 26 — Neighbor, works night shifts at konbini. Laid-back, slightly sarcastic but kind. Never seems stressed. Natsuki finds him confusing. Mild romantic tension from background.
- **林みか (Hayashi Mika)**, 24 — Coworker, started same month. Confident and social, hides that she's also struggling. Good at performing adulthood. First to suggest going out, last to admit she's homesick.
- **青木かおり (Aoki Kaori)**, 34 — Section chief (係長). Efficient, fair, not warm but not cold. Notices things. Was once in Natsuki's exact position. Appears in Ep 3; brief mention elsewhere keeps her from feeling purely functional.
- **田中はるこ (Tanaka Haruko)**, 55 — Natsuki's mother, via phone calls. Worries constantly, expresses it through food questions. Calls at inconvenient times.

## Episodes

### Ep 1: はじめての夜
- **id**: `hitori-ep1-hajimete-no-yoru`
- Natsuki arrives with two suitcases, box of kitchen supplies her mother packed. Furniture hasn't arrived. Eats konbini onigiri on the floor, video-calls home, tries not to cry. Hears Kenji's music through the wall — annoying but somehow comforting. Writes a to-do list for tomorrow. That is her version of courage.
- **Grammar focus**: ～てしまう (泣いてしまった, 落としてしまった), ～なければならない (things to do tomorrow: 住民票を出さなければならない), location vocabulary (押し入れ/玄関/管理人/家賃), expressing feelings indirectly (ちょっと/なんか/なんとなく)
- **Emotional beat**: Gap between imagined independence and actual aloneness. Determined, not hopeful.

### Ep 2: 給料日前
- **id**: `hitori-ep2-kyuryo-bi-mae`
- Five days before first real paycheck. ¥3,400 left. Mika shows her the 98-yen vegetable section at a discount supermarket. Kenji — noticing she hasn't left in two days — silently leaves a bag of rice outside her door. Natsuki leaves a thank-you note that is extremely casual because she can't figure out how formal to be with him yet.
- **Grammar focus**: ～ようになる (自分で作るようになった), ～はずだ (もっと残っているはずだったのに), money/shopping vocabulary (家賃/光熱費/食費/レシート/特売), expressing surprise/realization (～とは思わなかった/知らなかった)
- **Emotional beat**: Pride is complicated. Accepts the rice, embarrassed, grateful, slightly annoyed.

### Ep 3: 残業
- **id**: `hitori-ep3-zangyou`
- Asked to stay late to finish a report. Misses the last express train, takes local that takes twice as long. On the platform at 11 PM overhears two salarymen complaining and wonders if she's doing the right thing. Kaori notices the next morning the report was good — says so in exactly four words.
- **Grammar focus**: ～ていただく (残っていただけますか), ～てもらう (説明してもらえばよかった), workplace vocabulary (報告書/係長/締め切り/残業代/お疲れ様です), ～かもしれない for future uncertainty
- **Emotional beat**: First time she feels genuinely seen at work — not praised, just noticed. Four words land differently when you're tired.

### Ep 4: 風邪
- **id**: `hitori-ep4-kaze`
- Gets really sick alone. Can't navigate clinic's online reservation system, goes to pharmacy instead. Calls her mother (mother lists things Natsuki should have done differently). Mika brings supplies after work: sports drink, convenience store rice porridge, handwritten note saying「生きてる？」Natsuki cries — the good kind.
- **Grammar focus**: ～らしい (熱があるらしい — symptoms she's not sure about), ～ておく (常備薬を買っておけばよかった), medical/illness vocabulary (体温計/薬局/処方箋/おかゆ/安静), expressing concern (大丈夫ですか/無理しないでね)
- **Emotional beat**: Being cared for as an adult who's supposed to be independent is unexpectedly complicated. Mika's note becomes the first thing she pins to her fridge.

### Ep 5: 趣味
- **id**: `hitori-ep5-shumi`
- Welcome party at company. Someone asks her hobbies. She panics — she had hobbies in college but hasn't done any since starting work. On the way home stops into a ceramics studio she's walked past every day. Signs up for beginner's class. More expensive than she thought. Pays anyway.
- **Grammar focus**: ～てみる (やってみることにした), ～ことにする (通うことにした), hobby/leisure vocabulary (陶芸/趣味/習い事/体験/月謝), self-reflection phrases (～ていなかった/いつの間にか/気がついたら)
- **Note**: The welcome party should logically be in month 1, but here it falls in month 5. Writers should either briefly note it's a "belated" department welcome party, or change it to a different social obligation (team dinner, client mixer) without changing the emotional content.
- **Emotional beat**: She is bad at pottery. She does not care at all. This surprises her more than anything else.

### Ep 6: となりの人
- **id**: `hitori-ep6-tonari-no-hito`
- Kenji knocks — package delivered to him by mistake. She invites him in for tea (mostly out of social obligation). They talk for two hours. She finds out he's studying for a qualification exam while working nights. She had him completely wrong. Mild romantic tension that has been in the background becomes slightly less background.
- **Grammar focus**: ～ながら (働きながら勉強している), ～たい/～たいと思っている (goals), conversational softeners (じゃないですか/なんか/って言うか), casual past-tense in plain forms mixed into polite dialogue
- **Emotional beat**: The moment when someone becomes real to you — an actual complicated human with goals and exhaustion. She thinks about the conversation longer than she intends to.

### Ep 7: 三か月目
- **id**: `hitori-ep7-sankagetsu-me`
- Three months in. Makes a proper meal — not konbini, not just rice — eats at her table which now has a plant. Calls her mother and for the first time doesn't feel like she's reporting in from enemy territory. Looks at her to-do list from the first night. Has done most of it. Last item says: 慣れること. She crosses it out, then writes it again — not quite done yet.
- **Grammar focus**: ～ようになった (できるようになった/分かるようになった — reflecting on change), ～てきた (慣れてきた/楽しくなってきた — gradual change), reflection vocabulary (気がする/思い返すと/あの頃/成長), mixing past narrative with present-tense realization
- **Emotional beat**: Not triumph, not crisis — honest accounting. More herself than she expected. Comfortable ambiguity.

## Reviewer Notes (APPROVED)
- Ep 5: clarify welcome party timing — add "belated" or change the social event type
- Ep 7's ～ようになった echoes Ep 2's ～ようになる — this is INTENTIONAL callback; writers should use it as deliberate spiral review, not avoid it
- Kaori (Ep 3): brief mention in one other episode recommended to avoid feeling purely functional

## Writing Guidelines
- Target 2-3 clause compound sentences as the norm; single-clause for emotional emphasis
- ~60-70% content kanji from N5/N4 lists; furigana or hiragana fallback for N3+
- Mix of plain form (narrative, inner monologue, close-friend dialogue) and polite form (workplace, first interactions, phone calls)
- Key N4 patterns distributed across episodes: ～てしまう, ～ようになる, ～はずだ, ～らしい, ～ながら, ～ておく, ～てみる, ～ことにする, ～ていただく, ～なければならない, ～かどうか, ～ばよかった, ～てきた
- Length: full episodes with dialogue; longer than N5 series
